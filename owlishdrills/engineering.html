SPA Chart Chunks — Engineering Edition

30 chunks for the meetings engineers actually sit in — stand-ups, performance reviews, incident post-mortems, bug triage, delivery dates and technical trade-offs. Each card sets the scene first: read the chart, answer aloud, then check the target chunk.

How to run it solo:
  1. Read the meeting line, then the chart and the prompt. Say your answer aloud in a full sentence — record it on your phone.
  2. Write it or record it in the answer box, so you can compare your real wording with the model — speaking it aloud first is still the point.
  3. Tap Reveal. Compare with the target chunk, then try the Level-up line. Read the Watch out note — it covers the mistakes that change the meaning of a number.
  4. Mark yourself: ✓ nailed it · △ got it but slow/clunky · ✗ missed it. Re-say every △ and ✗ twice before moving on.
  5. Use the Section dropdown to drill one meeting type at a time.
  6. Download the PDF at the end — it includes everything you typed. Nothing is saved once you close the page.
